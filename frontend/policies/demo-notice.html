Academic demonstration notice

root/archive Lite is a separate university project, not the original production archive or an official university service.

Signing in uses Google Identity Services. The Worker verifies your Google account and stores its stable subject identifier privately with each contribution to enforce ownership. The optional display name, metadata and PDF are visible to anyone who can access the demo. Do not use real student records or personal information.

Upload only synthetic or authorized materials. Contributors and configured administrators can remove resources. Contact the project team about the demonstration and its data. The project team should remove demo materials after assessment.
