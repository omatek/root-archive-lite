The community behind the archive

About root

root is an independent, student-led engineering and IT community in Jordan. We bring curious people together to learn by doing, build real projects, share knowledge, and explore things beyond the classroom.

Through collaborative projects, workshops, experimentation, and competitions, we make room to try things out and learn from each other.

A little less starting from scratch.

root/archive Lite is a separate academic demonstration of a student resource archive, covering Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity at AUM. It uses independent cloud infrastructure and does not contain production submissions.

Anyone can browse and download. Eligible students can contribute PDF resources, which appear immediately. Contributors can edit or remove their own resources.

Built by a community.

Original student-authored notes, summaries, explanations, and original practice questions and test banks can make a difference. Share only material you have the right to share. Past examination papers are excluded.

root is an independent, student-led technology community based in Jordan. Neither root nor root/archive is operated, sponsored, or officially affiliated with AUM or another educational institution.

Use only synthetic or permitted materials for this classroom demonstration. Do not upload personal information.
